Parallel portfolio backtests by Newton iteration

September 2026

Portfolio backtests are typically sequential: each day's trades depend on the holdings left by the previous day. We explore a parallel approach using Newton's method to reconcile the full portfolio trajectory while evaluating daily investment decisions concurrently. Experiments with transaction costs and realistic portfolio constraints show close agreement with sequential backtests for long-only portfolios, while long-short portfolios remain more challenging.

Identifying a trading strategy from its return sequence

September 2026

Can a trading strategy be identified from its returns alone? We compare three methods—-squared-error matching, covariance analysis, and variance-weighted PCA—-to identify momentum, mean-reversion, risk-parity, and equal-weight strategies from daily return histories.
